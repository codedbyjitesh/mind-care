BSc IT Research Project

About Mind Care Portal

Empowering students through technology, self-reflection, and mental wellness tracking.

🎯 Project Objective

Mind Care is developed as an academic BSc Information Technology research project. It addresses the growing need for accessible, student-friendly self-care tools that help manage academic pressure, exam anxiety, and daily emotional balance.

🛡️ Privacy & Non-Clinical Scope

All student journals and mood logs are strictly private to the user. Mind Care serves as a wellness awareness portal and does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional psychological care.
