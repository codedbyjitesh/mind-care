Immediate Crisis Guidance

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger of self-harm or experiencing a severe psychological crisis, please contact your local emergency services (dial 112 in India, or your local emergency number) or proceed to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.

Important Medical Notice: "Mind Care is not a replacement for professional medical or psychological care."

How to Reach Out to Campus Support

Most university campuses have dedicated student welfare counselors. If you feel hesitant to talk, consider these practical steps:

1
Confide in a Trusted Friend or Faculty Member

Sharing your feelings out loud breaks the cycle of rumination.

2
Visit Your University Wellness Center

Campus counselors provide confidential, student-tailored sessions without academic penalty.

3
Use the 24/7 Tele-MANAS Service (14416)

Completely free, anonymous, and accessible anytime day or night.
